I'm Michael Roy, a DevOps engineer with 10+ years in IT and infrastructure operations. I work with Kubernetes, Linux, AWS, Bash, and automation, and I care about FOSS and self-hosting.

This is where I write about my homelab and what I'm learning. Most posts cover what broke, why it broke, and how I fixed it, along with the commands and configs that worked.

## Background

I supported production infrastructure as a data center technician at Oracle (June 2022 to February 2024) and as an IT specialist at TEKsystems (February 2021 to May 2022). Before that I served as a Military Police soldier in the US Army and later as an IT specialist in the Army National Guard. There I mainly ran the mobile Command Post Node racks and satellite terminal that carried NIPR and SIPR networks.

I now do freelance infrastructure work under Independent Infrastructure Labs. I work remotely from the US and Brazil.

## What I'm working on

My homelab is a 6 node HA k3s cluster with 3 control planes and 3 workers. I'm working toward GitOps on that cluster and studying for the CKAD exam.
